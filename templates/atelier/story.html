OUR STORY · OUR POINT OF VIEW

Clothing that
feels like you.

RUHAMAH is a contemporary womenswear label founded by Juliet Akoh, guided by a simple conviction: what we wear can help us feel fully ourselves.

THE HOUSE PHILOSOPHY

More than clothing.
An expression of identity.

At RUHAMAH, we believe that elegance is found in the details. Every garment represents an appreciation for craftsmanship, thoughtful design and the individuality of the woman who wears it.

From refined corporate silhouettes to contemporary English wear and beautifully interpreted African styles, our approach is centred on creating pieces that make women feel confident, comfortable and uniquely themselves.

Designed with intention.
THE CREATIVE VISION

Meet Juliet Akoh.

Founded by Juliet Akoh, RUHAMAH reflects a passion for fashion, creativity and the art of tailoring. Her vision is to create clothing that celebrates femininity, individuality and timeless elegance.
